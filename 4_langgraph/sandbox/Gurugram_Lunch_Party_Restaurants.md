# Budget Friendly Restaurants and Dhabas in Gurugram DLF Phase II for Lunch Party

Here are some budget-friendly restaurants and dhabas located in DLF Phase II, Gurugram, suitable for a lunch party for 5 individuals.

## 1. **Guftagu Cafe**
- **Rating:** 4.6/5
- **Best Cuisine:** North Indian, Cafe Style
- **Feedback:** This cafe is appreciated for its ambiance and decor, making it a good place for casual outings. Customers love the shakes and the overall vibe. The cozy seating and greenery create a pleasant dining experience.

## 2. **Dhaba by Claridges**
- **Rating:** 4.0/5
- **Best Cuisine:** Punjabi, North Indian
- **Feedback:** Offers a blend of good ambiance with traditional Indian food. Customers have praised the taste of dishes like tandoori chicken and fish, though some noted it is a bit on the pricier side compared to other dhabas.

## 3. **Zaffran**
- **Rating:** 4.2/5
- **Best Cuisine:** Mughlai, North Indian
- **Feedback:** Known for its authentic Mughlai dishes. Customers enjoy the biryanis and kebabs. It is revered for its rich flavors and generous portions, making it a favorite among locals.

## 4. **Bikanervala**
- **Rating:** 3.9/5
- **Best Cuisine:** North Indian, Vegetarian
- **Feedback:** A popular spot for vegetarian cravings. It offers a wide range of dishes from Indian snacks to full meals. Customers appreciate the quality of food and reasonable pricing.

## 5. **Nukkadwala**
- **Rating:** 4.0/5
- **Best Cuisine:** North Indian, Fast Food
- **Feedback:** Offers a casual atmosphere with a menu focused on Indian street food. Customers commend the taste and presentation, especially for the chat items and parathas.

---

You can choose any of these places depending on the cuisine preferences of your guests, and they all offer a good balance of quality and affordability!
