# Banoffee Pie Recipe

An easy family favorite with buttery pastry and sweet dulce de leche. Best served with a generous dollop of cream.

## Ingredients

### For the pastry
- 100g chilled butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 200g plain flour
- 1 medium egg, separated
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

### For the filling
- 4 sliced bananas
- 394g caramel or dulce de leche
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate (optional, for garnish)

## Method

### Step 1
Make the pastry case: put butter and flour in a food processor and pulse until it resembles fresh breadcrumbs. Add egg yolk and sugar and pulse again until mixed. Add cold water a tablespoon at a time until dough comes together.

### Step 2
Remove dough and gently knead until smooth. Wrap in cling film and chill for 30 minutes.

### Step 3
Heat oven to 190C/170C fan/gas 4 and grease a 23cm loose-bottomed tart tin. Roll out the pastry and line the tin, leaving half a cm proud of the edge.

### Step 4
Line base with baking parchment, add baking beans or rice, and bake for 15 minutes. Remove paper and beans, brush pastry with beaten egg white, bake for 15-20 minutes or until golden and cooked. Cool completely.

### Step 5
Spread half the caramel on the pastry case, layer sliced bananas, cover with the rest of caramel, and chill.

### Step 6
Whip the cream until thick and billowy, spoon over the top of the tart. Grate dark chocolate over to garnish if desired.

Serve and enjoy!
